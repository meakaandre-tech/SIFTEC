
# Recipes
## How recipes work
Every part follows Satisfactory's recipe: the same ingredients, on the machine that stands in for Satisfactory's. Amounts are cut down, because a Create line moves far fewer items per minute and milestone costs keep Satisfactory's counts.

Nodes give raw materials: raw iron, raw copper, raw zinc, coal, limestone, Nether quartz, bauxite, sulfur, SAM, and the fluids crude oil and nitrogen.
Hand crafting: until its machine unlocks, a part is crafted at a crafting table from the same ingredients.
Create's blocks keep Create's own crafting recipes, except seven that would otherwise need something from a later tier (Create's need brass, Electron Tubes or the Mechanical Crafter). These are crafting-table recipes from the pack's parts, and the originals are removed: Deployer (Rotor, Andesite Casing, Reinforced Iron Plate), Mechanical Crafter (Circuit Board, Steel Casing, crafting table; makes 3), Crushing Wheels (4 andesite alloy, 4 Steel Beam, 1 Rotor; makes 2), Flywheel (4 Iron Rod, 4 Iron Sheet, 1 shaft), Diesel Generators' Pumpjack crank (andesite alloy, iron sheets, zinc, shafts), Gunsmithing's Xeno-Basher (Reinforced Iron Plates, Rotor, Modular Frame, Iron Rod) and Nailgun (Reinforced Iron Plates, Copper Backtank, Iron Rod, Screw). The milestone or MAM node is what locks a block; locked by default means every item of Create, Diesel Generators, Gunsmithing and Hypertubes is either unlocked by a milestone or listed as free (the starting kinetics, raw and crushed materials, crafting ingredients and food).
Custom blocks and equipment are built at the Equipment Workshop, which takes a list of parts and has no grid.
Everything can be automated (owner's rule, 2026-10-10: "everything should be automatable, including powerlines"). Crafting-table recipes run in the vanilla Crafter and the Mechanical Crafter, both under the same unlocks as a player. The Workshop has an automatic mode, the Power Line is pressed from Cable, the small Workshop builds also have Mechanical Crafter recipes, and a Deployer strings Power Lines between poles (see Custom blocks and equipment).

## Custom blocks and equipment
These are built at the Equipment Workshop from a list of parts. Costs follow Satisfactory's build costs as I recall them where the building exists there, and are set for the pack otherwise.
Automation. Every build on this list can be made with no player:
- The Workshop's automatic mode. Right-click a build in the Workshop window to pick it (right-click again to stop). Belts, funnels, chutes and hoppers then feed the Workshop that build's parts. It only takes what the build still needs and holds at most one build's worth. When it has the whole cost it builds into its output slot, and funnels or hoppers take from there (or click the last slot of the window). It builds only what the company it belongs to has unlocked: the company of whoever placed it, or of the claim it stands in. Changing the pick gives the parts it holds back to the player. Left-click still builds by hand from the player's inventory.
- Power Line: a Mechanical Press turns 1 Cable into 1 Power Line, the same as the Workshop (HUB Upgrade 2; the Press comes at HUB Upgrade 3).
- Mechanical Crafter, at the same cost as the Workshop, for every build of 25 parts or fewer, one part per crafter on a grid of up to 5 by 5: Portable Miner, Node Scanner, Miner Mk.1, HUB, Claim Marker, HUB Engine, Power Pole, Power Tower, Parachute and Cardboard Drone. The bigger builds (up to 2,650 parts for the Wormhole Gateway) do not fit a crafter grid, so they use the Workshop's automatic mode. Cutting their costs to fit would change the balance.
- Stringing Power Lines: a Deployer holding Power Lines and used on a Power Pole or Tower joins it to the nearest pole in range that it is not joined to yet and that has a free line. That pole must be in the Deployer owner's claim or on unclaimed land. Each join uses one line, and a pole takes up to four. A player still joins two poles by clicking one and then the other.
Not made by machines: exploration pickups (Power Slugs, Mercer Spheres, Somersloops, Hard Drives). Machines can still use them: the Press makes Power Shards from slugs, and the Workshop's automatic mode takes a Mercer Sphere for a Dimensional Depot. Also not made by machines: what the AWESOME Shop sells (bought with points, the only source of those Create items) and Blueprints (saved by a player at the Blueprint Designer).

## Alternate recipes
The Hard Drive pool has 57 alternates, adapted from Satisfactory's. Each one is a second way to make a part the company already has; none replaces the standard recipe.
Offer: a researched drive shows two alternates the company does not own yet.
Eligibility: an alternate is only offered once its machine and every ingredient are unlocked, MAM trees included. An ingredient no milestone locks (crushed ore, a fluid) is traced back to the machine, fluid source or recipe that makes it, so Pure Iron Ingot waits for Crushing Wheels and Coated Cable for Oil Processing.
Yield: where "Makes" is higher than the standard recipe, that is the reward.
