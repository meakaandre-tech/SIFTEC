
# Recipes
## How recipes work
Every part follows Satisfactory's recipe: the same ingredients, on the machine that stands in for Satisfactory's. Amounts are cut down, because a Create line moves far fewer items per minute and milestone costs keep Satisfactory's counts.
Satisfactory machine
 | In the pack
 | What it can take
 |

 | Furnace, or Encased Fan over lava
 | One item
 |

 | Mechanical Press, Mechanical Saw, Millstone, Crushing Wheels, or Press over a Basin (compacting)
 | One kind of item
 |

 | Deployer
 | One base item on a belt or Depot, plus one item the Deployer holds
 |

 | Mechanical Mixer on a heated Basin
 | Two kinds of item
 |

 | Items and fluids

 | Items and two fluids
 | 
Particle Accelerator, Converter
 | Custom machines
 | Listed per recipe
 | 
Quantum Encoder
 | Sequenced assembly line
 | A base item and a fixed order of steps
 | 
Nodes give raw materials: raw iron, raw copper, raw zinc, coal, limestone, Nether quartz, bauxite, sulfur, raw uranium, SAM, and the fluids crude oil and nitrogen.
Hand crafting: until its machine unlocks, a part is crafted at a crafting table from the same ingredients.
Create's blocks keep Create's own crafting recipes. The milestone or MAM node is what locks them.
Custom blocks and equipment are built at the Equipment Workshop, which takes a list of parts and has no grid.

## Alternate recipes
The Hard Drive pool has 61 alternates, adapted from Satisfactory's. Each one is a second way to make a part the company already has; none replaces the standard recipe.
Offer: a researched drive shows two alternates the company does not own yet.
Eligibility: an alternate is only offered once its machine and every ingredient are unlocked, MAM trees included.
Yield: where "Makes" is higher than the standard recipe, that is the reward.
