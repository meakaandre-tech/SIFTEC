
# Wormhole Modpack — Design
 · 
## Overview
A Minecraft modpack that follows Satisfactory's progression, with Create as the tech base and a wormhole in place of the space elevator.
Version: Fabric 26.3, on Create Fly (the owner's 26.3 port) and the owner's 26.3 ports of Hypertubes, Diesel Generators and Gunsmithing.
Structure: Satisfactory's 10 tiers and milestones, with the same names (44: Tier 8's Nuclear Power is removed for now). Two tiers unlock per wormhole phase, and the finished wormhole opens a portal to The Sift.
Milestones: paid in parts at a company's HUB. Progression belongs to the company, not the player. After Tier 0, each one locks the HUB for its listed time.
Locked by default: every machine and recipe is locked until a milestone or MAM node unlocks it.
Resources: ore spawns normally for hand mining. Above-ground infinite nodes only produce with a miner on them.
Power: rotation only, with no electricity. Speed is capped per Logistics tier: 32, 64, 96, 128, 192, then 256 RPM.
Custom mod: one mod holds every pack feature. Ported mods stay pure ports.

### Crash sites
A crash site is a cargo pod thrown out of a failed rift, with wreckage around it. Each pod holds one Hard Drive.
Opening a pod: some open freely. Others need parts delivered, such as 20 Rotors, or a shaft connected with enough stress. Requirements get harder further from spawn.
Hard Drive: researched at the MAM for 10 minutes. It then offers a choice of two alternate recipes, drawn from the tiers the company has unlocked.
Alternate recipes: cheaper or different routes to existing parts. Examples: pure ingots by Fan washing, Cast Screw, Iron Wire, Stitched Iron Plate. The full pool of 57 is on the Recipes tab.
When the pool runs out: spare drives go in the AWESOME Sink for points.

## Nodes
Nodes sit on a jittered grid worked out from the world seed, so the scanner can find any node without its chunk being generated.
Grid: the world is split into cells of 128 by 128 blocks. A hash of the seed and the cell position decides whether the cell holds a node (about 60% do), where in the cell it sits, and its type and purity.
Biomes: looked up from the seed the same way, so biome rules need no generated chunks. No nodes spawn in oceans or rivers.
Start: world spawn is guaranteed one normal iron, copper and limestone node within 256 blocks.
One miner per node.

### Appearance
Nodes look like Satisfactory's: a patch of the resource breaking through the ground, not a block of ore.
Ore nodes: a low mound of cracked rock about 7 blocks across, with lumps and veins in the resource's colour and a flat centre where the miner sits. The blocks are unbreakable.
Oil: a dark, bubbling pool of crude that the Pumpjack stands over.
Nitrogen: a resource well, with a central vent and a ring of smaller vents hissing gas. The extractor goes on the central vent.
SAM: a cluster of dark purple crystals with a faint glow.
Purity: not visible on the node itself. Looking at a node or scanning it shows a label such as "Iron Ore, Pure".
## Claims
Companies claim land in chunks, and a node belongs to whoever has claimed the land under it.
HUB: placing a HUB claims the 5 by 5 chunks around it.
Claim Marker: a block that claims the 3 by 3 chunks around it. Markers can go on any unclaimed land, so a distant node can be claimed without owning everything in between.
Inside a claim: only company members can place or break blocks, open storage, and put miners on nodes.
Unclaimed land: open to everyone and not protected.
Budget: 4 markers at Tier 0 and 2 more per tier, so 22 at Tier 9. The budget is per company whatever its size, which is the second downside of a big company after the 50% cost increase.
Spacing: markers cannot go within 8 chunks of another company's HUB.
Chunk loading: claimed chunks stay loaded while at least one company member is online, so factories keep running. The claim budget is also the limit on what each company costs the server.
## Food
Food is carried over from the old design: there is no hunger, eating is the only way to heal, and 12 ingredients carry effects that grow stronger with preparation. It is built on Farmer's Delight.
No hunger bar and no natural regeneration: sprinting is free, and bigger meals heal more.
No death penalty.
Filling bases: rice, wheat, potatoes and meat have no effect of their own but heal a lot.

## Brass and heat
### Brass
Brass uses the flux chain from the old design and is the pack's Caterium. Create's default brass recipe is removed.
Crush raw copper and raw zinc in Crushing Wheels.
Mix crushed copper, crushed zinc and a flux into brass blend. No heat is needed.
Smelt the blend into brass, in a furnace or by Fan smelting.
Flux
 | Brass blend per mix
 | 
Sand
 | 1
 | 
Coal
 | 2
 | 
Sulfur
 | 3
 | 
Crushing Wheels unlock at Advanced Steel Production, so brass is reachable from Tier 4 by a company that finds zinc and researches the Caterium tree, which starts from crushed zinc. Quickwire is brass wire.
### Heat
Superheating is removed: Blaze Cake is disabled and a Blaze Burner only has its normal heat level.
Recipes: anything that asked for superheating becomes a heated recipe that also takes diesel as a fuel fluid, so it needs Tier 5.
Steel: Create: Gunsmithing's superheated steel recipe is replaced by the Foundry recipe from Basic Steel Production.
Boilers: every Blaze Burner counts as one unit of heat, so large boilers need more burners.
