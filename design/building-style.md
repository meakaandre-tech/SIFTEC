# Building style (Polartt's Satisfactory-in-Minecraft builds)

What the 12 reference shots in "SIFTEC Building Style References" have in common. `tools/structures.py` (`Frame`'s
style kit) and `prefab/Templates.java` follow this list; the client showroom (`worldtest style`) shoots every building
from the same three-quarter angle the references use.

1. **Materials.** Dark framed panels (Create industrial iron) are the body of every building; light framed panels
   (iron block) are the second grey; flat orange concrete is the colour. Framed glass shows the machines. Half-block
   detail comes from dark stairs and slabs (polished cut deepslate), light slabs and iron trapdoors, metal girders,
   industrial iron windows as vents and grilles, copper ladders and iron bars. Hazard stripes are yellow and black
   concrete. No wood, no brick, no stone textures on the buildings themselves.
2. **Trim and casing.** Walls read as a grid of panels, never one flat face: a dark plinth course at the bottom, a
   light panel band at the eaves (and often one at the sill), dark corner pillars, and vents or windows breaking up
   each face. Edges are finished with half blocks: stair skirts round the base, slab lips at the eaves, trapdoor
   sills under windows. Hazard stripes go on the feet and lower part of corner posts and legs, on bumpers at the
   front, and as L-shaped marks at the pad's corners.
3. **Machines framed and exposed.** The Create machine is the point: it sits behind big framed-glass windows or in
   an open bay under the roof, its top often showing through an opening in the hood. Moving parts are never boxed in
   completely; frames of dark pillars and light lintels surround them.
4. **Roofs and pillars.** Roofs step up: a flat panelled deck with a slab eave, then an orange hood over the machine
   whose sides slope down in dark stairs, and kit on top (vents, a connector, a small exposed housing). Tall
   buildings get dark square or girder pillars with orange bands and copper ladders; towers taper with stairs;
   arms and braces run on the diagonal in stairs and girders (the Space Elevator and derricks).
5. **Palette.** Mostly dark grey (about half), orange in large solid masses (about a third, hoods, shoulders, caps
   and pilasters rather than thin bands), light grey for bands and accents, glass and hazard yellow and black in
   small amounts. Everything stands on a light grey pad.
6. **Detail density.** High and even: every face is broken every two or three blocks, silhouettes step and are
   asymmetric (a tower, a stack or a hood off-centre), and nothing is a plain box. Small kit (vents, girders,
   ladders, rails) is spread over the whole building, not bunched on one side.
